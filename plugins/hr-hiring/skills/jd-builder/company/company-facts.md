These are the only company facts a JD may state. They are the same facts printed in the About us block (`company.json`). Anything about the role, team, tools, levels, ways of working or hiring process comes from the user's input and that run's research, not from this file.

- Onsurity Technologies, an employee healthcare benefits platform, based in Bengaluru.
- It offers SMEs, start-ups and enterprises flexible healthcare subscriptions covering discounted medicine, health checkups, fitness plans, free doctor teleconsultations and insurance in one membership.
- Plans cover full-time employees as well as contractual workers, interns, freelancers and consultants.
- Raised a $45M Series B led by Creaegis. Anil Kumble is a strategic advisor and investor. Featured in season one of Disney+ Hotstar's *The Great Indian Disruptors*. Named a Dun & Bradstreet Start-up 50 Trailblazer.
