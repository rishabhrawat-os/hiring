# Onsurity: facts agents may use

Only these facts, plus whatever HR supplies in the intake. If a JD needs a fact that isn't here, ask HR; don't search for it and don't guess. Source: the published SDE JDs, Oct 2026.

## The company
- Onsurity Technologies, Bengaluru. An employee healthcare benefits platform.
- It sells flexible healthcare subscriptions to SMEs, start-ups and enterprises: discounted medicine, health checkups, fitness plans, free doctor teleconsultations and insurance, all in one membership.
- It covers contractual workers, interns, freelancers and consultants, not only full-time employees. "Inclusion is the whole point."
- $45M Series B led by Creaegis. Anil Kumble is a strategic advisor and investor. Featured in season one of Disney+ Hotstar's *The Great Indian Disruptors*. Named a Dun & Bradstreet Start-up 50 Trailblazer.
- Web: onsurity.com. Social: LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube (links in `company.json`).

## How the work feels (from the JDs)
- Small enough that work is live within days, and consequential enough that a rushed decision is paid for by a dozen engineers and thousands of members.
- Product domains: claims, wallet, subscriptions, provider network, payments.
- The people who shape the work: insurance partners, regulators, hospitals, finance teams. They often don't agree with each other.
- The stakes: a member may be relying on the product during a hospital visit.
- Culture, the five non-negotiables:
  - high ownership;
  - highly motivated and self-driven;
  - extensive AI use;
  - fast, with a floor under it;
  - direct and low-ego.
- AI-assisted work is the default. Copilot, Claude and ChatGPT are in daily use, and AI is being built into the product.
- The interview loop judges evidence and judgement, not how many technologies a candidate can list.
